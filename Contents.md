Information from the Frahman & Brothers website
Overview
Frahman & Brothers describes itself as a fertilizer distributor serving farmers. The home page presents the company as “Your trusted partner in agricultural growth, supplying verified retailers with quality fertilizers for bountiful harvests”[1]. The company emphasizes reliability: it sources products directly from government godowns (public distribution warehouses), stores them in its own facilities and distributes them through verified local retailers[2]. The site’s content is organized through several navigation links (Home, About Us, Our Products, Our Supply Chain, Contact Us and a Sample Page). A blog section exists but only contains a default WordPress post.

Our Supply Chain
The supply‑chain page describes how Frahman & Brothers manages product sourcing and distribution. It breaks the process into three steps:
Direct sourcing from government godowns – the company procures fertilizers directly from official government depots to eliminate counterfeit products and ensure that each batch meets national quality standards[26].
Secure transportation & storage – after procurement, products are transported in dedicated covered vehicles and stored in the company’s modern, secure godowns. These facilities maintain optimal conditions, preventing moisture damage and preserving product efficacy[27].
Distribution to verified local retailers – Frahman & Brothers distributes fertilizers exclusively to pre‑verified retail stores within its operating area. This structured approach ensures consistent supply, preserves product integrity and gives farmers reliable local access to genuine fertilizers and advice[28].
The page explains that end‑to‑end control over procurement, storage and distribution allows the company to guarantee product quality and support agriculture in its community[29].

Additional observations
Consistent branding: The footer across all pages repeats the contact details and tagline “Your Trusted Partner in Agricultural Growth”[7]. This consistent placement ensures that visitors can easily find the company’s location, phone and email.
Incomplete content: Some sections contain placeholder text (e.g., the “You Should Choose Us” bullet points on the home page and the DAP section under products). These areas should be edited with substantive information to better reflect the company’s operations[38][25].
Website structure: The navigation menu appears twice (primary and secondary) on most pages, and the design suggests the site is built on WordPress. Beyond the navigation pages and the single blog post, no other substantial content or downloadable resources were found.
Overall, Frahman & Brothers’ website portrays a fertilizer distributor focused on quality, reliability and local agricultural support. The pages outline the company’s mission, supply‑chain practices, products and contact details, although parts of the site remain under construction or include placeholder text.
