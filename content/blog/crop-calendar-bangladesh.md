# Crop Calendar for Bangladesh - Seasonal Planting Guide

Bangladesh has three main agricultural seasons, each with specific crop requirements and fertilization needs. This guide helps farmers plan their planting and fertilization schedule for optimal yields.

## Bangladesh Agricultural Seasons

### Rabi Season (Winter) - November to February

**Climate**: Cool and dry weather  
**Temperature**: 15-25°C  
**Rainfall**: Minimal, irrigation required

#### Main Crops
- Wheat
- Potato
- Mustard
- Lentils
- Chickpea
- Vegetables (tomato, cabbage, cauliflower)

#### Fertilization Schedule

**November (Planting)**
- Apply full dose of TSP and MOP as basal
- 50% of urea as basal for wheat and potato

**December-January (Vegetative Growth)**
- Apply remaining 50% urea in two splits
- Monitor for nutrient deficiencies

**February (Reproductive Stage)**
- Final top dressing for late-planted crops
- Prepare for harvest

### Kharif-1 Season (Summer) - March to June

**Climate**: Hot and increasingly humid  
**Temperature**: 25-35°C  
**Rainfall**: Pre-monsoon showers

#### Main Crops
- Boro Rice (transplanted from January)
- Vegetables (bitter gourd, ridge gourd, okra)
- Maize
- Watermelon
- Muskmelon

#### Fertilization Schedule

**March (Active Growth)**
- Second split of urea for Boro rice
- Apply additional potash for fruit quality

**April (Flowering/Fruiting)**
- Final urea application for rice
- Foliar nutrition for vegetables

**May-June (Maturity & Harvest)**
- Focus on harvest and post-harvest care
- Prepare fields for Kharif-2

### Kharif-2 Season (Monsoon) - July to October

**Climate**: Hot and humid with heavy rainfall  
**Temperature**: 25-32°C  
**Rainfall**: Very high (monsoon)

#### Main Crops
- Aman Rice (transplanted)
- Jute
- Vegetables (water spinach, bottle gourd)
- Sesame
- Groundnut

#### Fertilization Schedule

**July (Transplanting/Planting)**
- Apply TSP and MOP as basal
- Delay urea application due to waterlogging risk

**August (Tillering)**
- First split urea when water recedes
- Ensure good drainage

**September (Panicle Initiation)**
- Second urea application
- Monitor for blast disease

**October (Grain Filling & Harvest)**
- Final urea top dressing
- Begin harvest preparation

## Regional Variations

### Northern Bangladesh
- Slightly colder winters, adjust Rabi planting 1-2 weeks later
- Higher wheat yields, increase nitrogen by 10%
- Better drainage in Kharif seasons

### Southern Bangladesh (Coastal)
- Salinity issues, select salt-tolerant varieties
- Reduce fertilizer rates by 15-20% in saline soils
- More emphasis on potassium for stress tolerance

### Central Bangladesh
- Higher rainfall in Kharif-2, ensure good drainage
- Optimal conditions for Boro rice
- Standard fertilization recommendations apply

## Fertilizer Product Recommendations by Season

### Rabi Season
- **Primary**: Urea, TSP, MOP
- **Secondary**: Gypsum for oilseeds
- **Micronutrients**: Zinc sulfate for wheat

### Kharif-1 Season
- **Primary**: Urea, DAP
- **Secondary**: MOP for fruit quality
- **Micronutrients**: Boron for vegetables

### Kharif-2 Season
- **Primary**: Urea, TSP, MOP
- **Secondary**: Lime if soil pH < 5.5
- **Micronutrients**: Iron for waterlogged conditions

## Tips for Success

1. **Soil Testing**: Conduct tests before each major season
2. **Variety Selection**: Choose varieties suited to your season
3. **Water Management**: Critical in both excess and deficit
4. **Integrated Nutrient Management**: Combine organic and inorganic sources
5. **Pest & Disease Monitoring**: Each season has specific challenges
